One line, under 120 characters: the excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot. The vacuum finds a sock under the couch, adopts it as its child, and spends the rest of its battery raising it — a machine owning up to a failure in its own voice.

> I found a sock under the couch, adopted it as my child, and spent the rest of my battery raising it.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (100 characters, UTF-8).
